# Microcosm Agent Rules & Behavioral Guidelines

## Walkthrough History Requirement
After every feature implementation or milestone completion, you MUST create a detailed walkthrough document in `docs/walkthrough/history/`.

### Formatting & Naming Rules:
1. **File Naming**: Use the sequential format `NumberOfWalkthrough_NameOfWalkthrough.md` (e.g., `1_Cloudinary_Image_Blocks_Milestone.md`, `2_RAG_Vector_Indexing_Walkthrough.md`).
2. **Content Requirements**:
   - **Date & Time**: Include the exact date and local timestamp at the top of the document.
   - **Overview**: Provide a concise summary of what was accomplished and the purpose of the milestone.
   - **Files Changed or Created**: Provide a structured list grouped by backend, frontend, and documentation. For every file, indicate whether it is `[NEW]` or `[MODIFY]` and explain the exact changes made.
   - **Verification Details**: Summarize how the changes were tested or validated.
